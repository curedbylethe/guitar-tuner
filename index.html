    Method

    Pitch is found with YIN difference analysis on a 50 ms interval and shown in cents from the target string. In tune means within 5 cents and steady. Once a string reads in tune, it stays in tune until it drifts past 8 cents, so a small wobble does not make the label flicker. Custom mode keeps the equal-tempered intervals of the base tuning and moves them to the string you record.

    Pitch is detected on this device from the microphone. No audio is stored or sent. Concert pitch is A4 = 440 Hz. Detection accuracy has not been checked against a calibrated reference tuner.
